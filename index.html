SUPPORTED

A reproducible shared pipeline

All fourteen variants are preserved. The latest release provides 193 complete historical paths: 188 shared-model estimates and five inherited fallbacks. Six-country excluded-fold errors are reproducible from saved predictions.

UNRESOLVED

Global target accuracy

Six eligible weak references cannot represent the global stock. There are zero untouched, target-aligned all-use GFA validation countries in this snapshot. Fitted feature ranges and leverage flags do not establish an Area of Applicability.

UNRESOLVED

Definition and uncertainty

Physical floorspace proxies and local statistical GFA definitions differ. Gross completions, demolitions and vertical change are not separately identified. No GFA confidence interval has calibrated coverage.

CURRENT RESEARCH JUDGMENT

Retain the default. Preserve the candidates.

v0.13 has the lowest mean history error among the ten complete candidates on the matched six-country diagnostic. v0.12 is more stable on several accompanying diagnostics. v0.3 remains better on the partial Korean transfer check. The evidence supports continued research, not adoption of a globally superior model.

Reference dates, stock scopes and global coverage diagnostics

Reference handling fixed before scoring

Denmark's 1 January stock for year y+1 approximates the preceding 31 December. The date rule was fixed from source metadata, not selected for performance. Finland's register/classification bridge remains unresolved and excludes it from fitting. Korean 2015 is quarantined for incompatible source revisions.

Global coverage

Five fallback countries: ERI, LIE, PRK, SSD and YEM. PSE remains missing and VAT retains only 2020. 182 countries have at least one out-of-training-range feature flag; this is a predictor diagnostic, not validated AOA. In v0.13, 46 cumulative and 103 annual transfer corrections are attenuated by the shared support rule. No release demonstrates global interval coverage.
