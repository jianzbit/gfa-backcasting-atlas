02 / HOW IT IS CALCULATED

Two building stocks.
One consistent total.

The model estimates annual net stock change using shared global rules. It starts with a fixed 2020 physical floor-area proxy and integrates residential and non-residential trajectories backward and forward.

01

Set the reference level

The existing 2020 anchor converts TEMPO footprint and volume into physical floorspace using three fixed conversion models, weighted 45%, 25% and 30%. This level is preserved; it is not assumed to be measured truth.

R₂₀₂₀ = (1 − q) A₂₀₂₀
N₂₀₂₀ = q A₂₀₂₀

q is a conditional non-residential volume-share allocation. A shared peer-median rule handles missing qualified composition. Separate sector areas have additional uncertainty.

02

Estimate shared annual changes

Quality-qualified inputs are scaled on the training countries, bounded, and pooled into demand, production, background and annual timing mechanisms. Seven regularized coefficients are shared across all countries.

gᴿᵢ,ₜ = hᵢ,ₜ + 0.01 xᵢ,ₜ β
gᴺᵢ,ₜ = gᴿᵢ,ₜ + 0.01 βᴺ dₜ

h is the horizontal surface-change prior. x includes six pooled coordinates and support-aware transfer weights. d = 1 through 2020, then 0.8ᵗ⁻²⁰²⁰.

03

Integrate the two stocks

Annual log changes compound from the 2020 level. The total always equals residential plus non-residential floorspace. Annual net change is the difference between adjacent year-end stocks.

Rₜ = Rₜ₋₁ exp(gᴿₜ)
Nₜ = Nₜ₋₁ exp(gᴺₜ)
Totalₜ = Rₜ + Nₜ

Backcasting uses the inverse accumulation before 2020. Gross construction and demolition are not separately identified by these net changes.

Fitted parameters, shared rules and interval formulas

Frozen shared parameters

Six common coefficients have ridge penalty 1; the sector departure has penalty 9. Country-balanced robust fitting uses seven development countries. No fitted national correction coefficient is added.

Missing inputs and extrapolation

Source gaps stay missing. Unsupported mechanisms are omitted; explicit shared horizontal and composition fallbacks are used where needed. Predictor-range flags identify extrapolation, not validated reliability scores.

04 / RESEARCH STATUS

Useful estimates.
Visible limits.

The complete research grid is available. The evidence does not yet establish a globally optimal model or a global absolute-accuracy percentage.

DELIVERED

Annual country estimates

195 country series from 2015 to 2030, with residential and non-residential components. The primary global sum includes 194 countries. Palestine is a separately labeled source-conditioned supplement.

TESTED WITH LIMITED REFERENCES

Historical relative change

Seven-country whole-country-exclusion refits and two reserved-country native-stock diagnostics are preserved. The native reference definitions and prior development exposure limit their interpretation.

Simple controls perform better on some tests. Full-fit country paths on this page are not held-out validation.

UNRESOLVED

Absolute accuracy & future outcomes

The 2020 anchor conversion, sector split and global representativeness need independent evidence. The 37 scenario range is sensitivity, not validated probability coverage. 2030 outcomes are not observed yet.
