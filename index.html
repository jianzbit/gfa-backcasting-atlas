02 / HOW IT IS CALCULATED

Two building stocks.
One consistent total.

The shared model supplies the existing Total GFA trajectory. Residential and non-residential areas are now allocated using qualified GHSL volume changes at 2015, 2020, 2025 and 2030, preserving every Total and the 2020 sector anchors.

01

Keep the reference level

The 2020 anchor converts TEMPO footprint and volume into physical floorspace using three fixed conversion models, weighted 45%, 25% and 30%. All 2020 Total, Residential and Non-residential values are preserved.

R₂₀₂₀ = (1 − q₀) A₂₀₂₀
N₂₀₂₀ = q₀ A₂₀₂₀

q₀ is the inherited conditional non-residential allocation, based on qualified source volume shares or a shared peer median. It is not measured GFA composition.

02

Retain the shared Total path

The v16 model uses qualified population, economic, construction, remote sensing and context factors with seven shared, regularized coefficients. Its existing Total GFA at every country and year is retained.

Aₜ = retained v16 Totalₜ

The total combines the original two-state trajectories. GHS-WUP provides the future horizontal path with damped historical corrections. No new fitting, country-specific coefficient or extra GHSL vote is added to Total growth.

03

Apply the GHSL sector changes

Normalize residential-complement and non-residential volumes to their own source 2020. Transfer their relative changes to the inherited GFA split, then allocate the retained Total.

mRₜ = Vᴿₜ / Vᴿ₂₀₂₀
mNₜ = Vᴺₜ / Vᴺ₂₀₂₀
qₜ = q₀ mNₜ / [(1 − q₀) mRₜ + q₀ mNₜ]
Nₜ = Aₜ qₜ   ·   Rₜ = Aₜ − Nₜ

191 historical and 189 future country paths use this allocation. Intermediate volumes use log interpolation, or linear interpolation at a structural zero. Missing qualified bundles retain v16; missing values are never zero-filled.

Sector source: EC JRC GHS-BUILT-V R2023A, cubic metres per native cell at five-year epochs, with 2018 height held constant. Predominant-building NRES and its residential complement are volume categories, not observed floor-function GFA. Product methods: Pesaresi et al. (2024).

A different information basis in each period

2015–2019Historical backcast

The retained Total uses historical factors. The sector split uses qualified GHSL 2015/2020 volume endpoints with annual interpolation.

2020Fixed physical proxy

The inherited Total and both sector areas are unchanged. Anchor and conversion uncertainty carry into the full trajectory.

2021–2026Reconstruction / nowcast

The retained Total uses GHS-WUP and damped historical corrections. Sector shares interpolate GHSL volume paths involving projected 2025/2030 epochs; these are not annual use observations.

2027–2030Conditional forecast

The retained Total continues its fixed shared rules. Sector shares follow the GHSL 2025/2030 projected volume changes; future height and actual building-use conversions remain unobserved.

Fitted parameters, shared rules and interval formulas

Frozen shared parameters

Six common coefficients have ridge penalty 1; the sector departure has penalty 9. Country-balanced robust fitting used seven development countries. These frozen coefficients now supply the retained Total path; the GHSL allocation adds zero fitted parameters.

TESTED WITH LIMITED REFERENCES

Historical relative change

The adopted allocation has mixed historical results. Seven-country residential holdout disagreement rises from 1.735 to 1.804; six nontraining-country five-year disagreement falls from 4.389 to 4.343, and LVA/NLD falls from 0.639 to 0.544. Lower is better.

This is a source-informed composition update, not an established accuracy improvement. References were previously viewed and native definitions differ from GFA. Full-fit country paths are not held-out validation.

UNRESOLVED

Absolute accuracy & future outcomes

The 2020 anchor conversion, sector split and global representativeness need independent evidence. All 37 scenarios were reallocated consistently before country/world ranges were recomputed. The range is conditional sensitivity, not validated probability coverage. 2030 outcomes are not observed yet.
