Testing nonlinear growth in B0

Indicator 1.1 estimates annual residential, non-residential and total building floorspace for 2015–2030. This study asks whether a small nonlinear extension can improve the existing B0 growth model while preserving its 2020 anchor and sector allocation.

Finding: the tested nonlinear responses did not produce a consistent improvement. B0 remains the default research baseline.

Retain operational B0. Preserve all four research comparisons; no automatic model promotion or per-country model picking.

Complete refits precede scoring. The same exposed panels have informed previous development; these results are not an untouched final test or a global accuracy score.

Reading the metric: if the official starting stock is 100 m², an observed increase of 2 m² and a modeled increase of 3 m² produce a +1 percentage-point increment error. The reported MAE averages the absolute errors within each country, then gives countries equal weight.

What exactly is nonlinear?

B0 already compounds logarithmic rates and uses bounded transformations. The new part is a bend in the response to annual activity and demand departures: below-normal and above-normal inputs need not have the same slope.

gᴿ(c,t) = h(c,t) + 0.01 × [x(c,t)ᵀβ + γA H(A(c,t)) + γU H(U(c,t))]
gᴺ(c,t) = gᴿ(c,t) + 0.01 × βN d(t)
H(x) = [max(−clip(x), 0) − a × clip(x)] / s
R(t) = R(t−1) × exp(gᴿ(t)); N(t) = N(t−1) × exp(gᴺ(t))
Total(t) = R(t) + N(t)   ·   fixed 2020 anchor

The knot is zero departure from a country's historical normal, not zero GDP growth. Training-only a removes overlap with its own linear coordinate; it does not establish independence from every other input; s normalizes the remaining hinge. H(0)=0. Only the added response is clamped outside training support; every such case is flagged.

Only DNK supplies eligible post-2020 stock labels in the unchanged training deck. That severely limits learning how later shocks affect building stocks. New GDP or investment observations are predictors, not extra GFA labels.

COVID and war: distinguish responsiveness from demonstrated accuracy.

Observed annual economic inputs can move the updated axes; a synthetic future-input intervention verifies that the implementation responds only from the affected year onward. That establishes code behavior, not actual crisis accuracy. GDP contraction is not equivalent to building destruction. Post-2020 conflict/damage inputs were not refreshed, and the current reference panel cannot establish war-specific skill.

Later years without qualified observations retain explicit damped continuation; future crises are not known. Latest-vintage predictors and WUP projections do not constitute a cutoff-valid forecast hindcast.

Three levels of evidence

Engineering: unchanged anchor, R + NR = Total, annual increments, protected baseline hashes, identical-input controls and held-label poisoning tests.

Development diagnostics: country-excluded native stock changes and historical RES/Total channels, reported separately. External source provenance is retained; this experiment does not add a new cross-product GFA certification.

Independent scientific validation: insufficient evidence for a global accuracy percentage, crisis-specific accuracy or genuine future forecast accuracy. No Spec PASS is inferred from this exploratory panel.

Methods and primary references

The fixed one-knot response follows the regression-spline construction in Forecasting: Principles and Practice. The mgcv documentation explains the distinction between basis size and effective degrees of freedom. This is a custom low-rank penalized B0 extension, not an mgcv fit. The references support the method design, not a claim of improved GFA accuracy.
