Feature screen

Historical intervals: 2016–2020. Reference targets are adjacent-year native stock growth. Each test country is excluded from fitting and standardization; countries have equal total weight. Source values are qualified before use. No missing inputs are filled with zero.

ŷ = ȳtrain + sy,train b z
b = Σ(w z y*) / [Σ(w z²) + 1]

Fixed ridge penalty 1. At least three training countries and three matched intervals in the held country. This is a retrospective one-feature screen, not an origin-valid forecast or full-model feature ablation. Annual Pearson r uses within-country demeaning; source preparation includes B0 lags and risk-sign conventions.

Weight experiment

A single discrete shared choice ρ∈{0,0.5,1}. Inner country exclusion selects the option; the outer country is then scored. Every label-dependent fit, normalization and leverage calculation excludes the outer and inner countries as applicable. Qualified unlabeled composition donors also exclude them.

Anchor, source masks, within-family quality weights, sector allocation, seven coefficient penalties and continuation rules are unchanged. The original options were not expanded after scores.

Absolute log-ratio error selects the pooling option; one-year and five-year stock-increment errors are separate evaluation metrics. These objectives need not rank every candidate identically.

Stock-increment metric

ec,t,k = 100 [(Ŝc,t+k − Ŝc,t) − (Sc,t+k − Sc,t)] / Sc,t

k=1 for annual changes; k=5 for five-year changes. Report MAE and signed bias in percentage points. Shared 2020 reference levels support a conditional trajectory comparison; they do not validate the original absolute anchors. Predeclared Finnish 2019–2020 and Korean 2015–2016 publisher breaks are excluded. Overlapping intervals are not new independent countries.

Adjustments supported for further investigation

  • Retain qualified annual signals within physical mechanisms; avoid extra independent votes for related variables.
  • Separate static PPP, urban fraction, interest and morphology context from annual timing.
  • Prioritize fresh post-2020 observations over additional similar proxies. Comparable completed-floor-area or stock-change records can add information; completions still measure additions rather than net change.
  • Keep candidate household counts and building-change inputs unavailable until comparable qualified data are acquired. Disaster-affected people are not destroyed floor area.

No new source datasets were acquired in this run. The original 43 inventory excludes six additional OBAT static attributes and the separate WUP horizontal path. These remain documented auxiliary inputs; their presence does not change the F01–F43 count.
