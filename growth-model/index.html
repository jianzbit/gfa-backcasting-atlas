Step 1 · Prepare the information

Pool related factors before fitting.

The model does not fit 43 separate factor weights. It first organizes qualified inputs into mechanism families, then combines those families into a small set of model coordinates. Different countries use the qualified inputs available to them.

Building morphology, development level, stock age, household context and risk.

Qualifying context modifies the demand/production relationship through an interaction, J. Static values do not become newly observed annual changes.

A: annual activity departures

Uses within-country deviations in land observation, construction, investment and lights. It describes whether a year is stronger or weaker than that country's historical average.

U: annual demand departures

Uses within-country deviations in demography, migration, GDP, sector activity, financial conditions and risk. These terms help shape the timing of the historical trajectory.

Two kinds of weights: fixed quality/family weights combine the inputs into pools; fitted coefficients determine how the resulting coordinates adjust log growth. These are different operations.

See normalization, pooling, lag and missing-data rules
  1. Historical input window: the frozen annual feature intake uses 2016–2020 intervals. Annual-source status alone does not mean an input supplies usable annual variation.
  2. Period signals: non-static factors need at least three finite historical intervals for a country summary. Static context uses the final qualified historical context once. Training-country support and a nonzero scale are required.
  3. Normalization: period signals are scaled using training-country summaries and bounded with tanh. Static levels are also centered on the training-country mean. Annual signals are centered within country, scaled using training-country annual variation, bounded, locally smoothed with relative weights 1–2–1 over available neighbors, and recentered.
  4. Lag handling: construction value-added combines current and one-year lagged growth with fixed 50/50 weights, requiring both values. Other pipeline signals combine the current and preceding available annual interval, also 50/50; the first unsupported interval remains missing.
  5. Quality weights: qualified globally usable or geographically limited signals receive weight 1; contextual/proxy signals receive 0.25; unsuitable signals receive 0. Family weights are 1 for demography; 0.5 for land observation, construction and investment; 0.25 for the other families. These are fixed working rules, not calibrated probabilities.
  6. Available-member pooling: a missing raw measurement stays missing and is omitted from its pool. At the model-operator stage, an unavailable coordinate may contribute zero correction; this does not assert that the missing population, investment or other measurement is zero.
  7. Support attenuation: the pooled correction is reduced when design leverage exceeds the training-country support. The rule is shared; it has not independently established prediction accuracy outside that support.

Related OBAT height and age attributes are pooled so that several variants from the same source do not each receive a separate mechanism vote.

These coefficients act on normalized pooled coordinates, then on a 0.01 rate scale. They are neither raw-factor elasticities nor causal effects. A negative pooled coefficient does not establish that every raw member reduces GFA.

Where does the built-surface prior come from?

Through 2020: normally the legacy GHSL 2015–2020 endpoint mean, h = log(surface2020 / surface2015) / 5. It is a multi-year average repeated across annual intervals, not five independent annual building observations.

After 2020: a qualified GHS-WUP built-surface path supplies annual log changes. Source epochs and their interpolation/projection provide that path; they do not measure a new building height every year.

Fallbacks: when the historical prior is absent, use a qualified WUP historical endpoint ratio, otherwise the shared training-country median. When the future WUP path lacks the required support, retain the historical horizontal prior.

Built surface measures horizontal occupation. It is not floor area, and its fixed coefficient in B0 is an assumption before the learned adjustment.

What exactly changes after 2020?

The historical demand, production and interaction coordinates are retained. Annual-variation coordinates are seeded from their final three historical years. The entire factor-correction design is then damped by 0.8t−2020. The horizontal prior remains separate.

What this does not mean: GFA is not reduced by 20% each year. The original B0 does not ingest new annual GDP, investment, nighttime-light or observed shock data after 2020. The 2023 TEMPO update belongs to a separate P1 research experiment; it is not silently applied to this frozen B0.

Why a 2020–2023 surface/volume observation is not automatically a GFA growth rate

A building product may change because of true expansion, vertical growth, detection, effective support or conversion assumptions. Even comparable footprint or volume change is not directly a recorded change in usable or gross floor area. B0's horizontal prior and the P1 evidence-update experiment must therefore remain distinguishable.

Similarly, modeled GHSL/WUP epochs and their annual interpolation are source-informed paths, not independent annual GFA observations.

Step 5 · Learn a shared relationship

What was fitted—and what was fixed?

Fitted from the development panel

  • Seven shared growth coefficients.
  • Training-only input scales and support statistics.
  • Fit to 53 non-anchor relative-change observations across Chile, Germany, Denmark, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, South Korea and Romania.

These are seven country clusters, not 53 independent countries. Native reference channels support the matching residential or Total component; they do not all measure the full global target definition.

Fixed working assumptions

  • 2020 area anchor and conditional initial split.
  • Feature/family pooling, quality rules and lag structure.
  • Rate scale 0.01 and future damping 0.8.
  • Ridge penalties: 1 for the first six coefficients; 9 for the non-residential contrast.

These settings are not an empirical accuracy guarantee. Fitting growth does not independently calibrate the absolute 2020 anchor.

Open the training target and objective

The reference target is the recorded source's log ratio to its own 2020 value:

yi,t = log(Referencei,t / Referencei,2020)

The model integrates annual rates to produce the matching residential, non-residential or nonlinear Total ratio. It minimizes robust country-block residuals plus the fixed ridge penalty. Source/channel budgets prevent a country with many records from automatically dominating; shared-2020 dependence is handled in the working covariance.

Objective = Σ country-block robust loss + ½ Σ penaltyⱼ × βⱼ²

The working log-ratio residual scale is 0.05, and the robust country-block threshold is 1.35. These are fitting settings, not published accuracy percentages or confidence limits.

Training is not the same as validation. In a genuine held-country-out evaluation, the excluded country's labels must be removed from fitting and all label-dependent preprocessing. A full-fit country path cannot be relabeled as an independent test.

Cutoff matters: the frozen full fit includes development references through 2025. Its 2021–2025 output is not a forecast that was available in 2020. A genuine historical forecast test must rerun the complete permitted learning process using only information available at the forecast origin.

Why not simply add more factors or use a separate coefficient for each country?

With seven independent development countries, many free coefficients would be weakly determined. Fixed pooling and regularization reduce the number of fitted responses. A shared equation still produces different country paths because the country inputs differ. It does not prove that the relationship transfers to every country.

B0 is a low-dimensional shared growth model with nonlinear input bounding, an interaction and exponential stock accumulation. It is not the Landsat gradient-boosted B2 model, and it is not a literal straight-line interpolation between two GFA endpoints.

EXTERNAL CONSISTENCY DIAGNOSTICS

Do products broadly agree?

Comparisons with MaterialCities, GEM, OBM, OSM or other building products can reveal scale, coverage and composition differences. Shared inputs and different definitions prevent disagreement from being treated automatically as model error.
