B0 Growth Model: Mathematical Specification

Indicator 1.1 · Frozen v16 implementation · 2015–2030

The model estimates annual net stock changes using a built-surface prior and a shared regression on normalized factor pools. Residential and non-residential stocks are integrated separately from a fixed 2020 anchor.

Notation: i = country; t = interval end-year; k = factor; f = mechanism family; R = residential; NR = non-residential. Annual rates g are log changes, so ordinary growth is exp(g) − 1.

1. Transform and normalize the factors

Let ri,t,k denote a qualified, transformed factor after any specified lag treatment. The historical input window is 2016–2020. The original inventory has 43 candidates; 22 have direct annual-timing use somewhere in the frozen intake. Six additional static OBAT attributes enter the relevant background families where qualified.

Period summary

si,k = meant∈H(i,k)(ri,t,k)dynamic factor; at least 3 finite historical intervalssi,k = ri,2020,kstatic context; requires the final historical value
zi,k = tanh[(si,k − ck) / σk]

H(i,k) contains the country's finite qualified historical intervals for factor k. For static context, ck is the training-country mean; for dynamic summaries, ck = 0. The scale σk is the training-country root mean square around ck. Eligibility requires positive quality weight, support from at least three training countries and a nonzero scale.

Annual timing signal

ui,t,k = tanh[(ri,t,k − r̄i,k) / τk] ai,t,k = Centert{Smooth1,2,1(ui,t,k)}

Here r̄i,k is the country's finite historical mean, and τk is estimated from centered annual variation in the training countries. Smoothing uses available neighboring years, with the current year weighted twice. Static factors do not generate annual timing signals.

Construction growth uses 0.5 × current + 0.5 × one-year lagged growth. Other pipeline signals use the current and preceding interval, also 50/50; both must be available. Missing raw observations remain missing.

2. Construct the factor pools

Available normalized factors are pooled within mechanism families. Family summaries are then pooled into model coordinates. Both stages use a weighted mean over available members:

Fixed quality weights are 1 for usable sources, 0.25 for contextual/proxy sources and 0 for unsuitable sources. Fixed family weights are 1 for demography; 0.5 for land observation, construction and investment; and 0.25 otherwise. These pooling weights are distinct from the fitted regression coefficients.

OBAT height variants are combined before the morphology pool; age variants are combined before the stock-age pool. An unavailable model coordinate may contribute zero correction, but the missing source measurement is never assigned an observed zero.

3. Define the shared annual-growth equations

The six residential correction coordinates are an intercept, two period summaries, one background interaction and two annual departures:

xi,t = [1, Di, Pi, Ji, Ai,t, Ui,t]T x̃i,t = κixi,t,   t ≤ 2020 gRi,t = hi,t + 0.01 x̃i,tTβ1:6 gNRi,t = gRi,t + 0.01 βNRdt

The seven coefficients are shared across countries. The support factor κi = min(1, Lmax,train/Li) attenuates the correction when a country's design leverage exceeds training support. It equals 1 when no attenuation is needed.

G = [(1/5) Σi∈train XiTXi + I6]−1Li = trace(Xi G XiT)/5,   Lmax,train = maxi∈trainLiXi stacks the five unattenuated historical design rows, with unavailable model coordinates masked to zero.

Horizontal prior h

hi,t = log(Surfacei,2020 / Surfacei,2015) / 5,   t ≤ 2020hi,t = log(WUPSurfacei,t / WUPSurfacei,t−1),   t > 2020

The historical expression normally uses the legacy GHSL endpoint trend. If absent, a qualified WUP historical ratio is used, otherwise the shared training median. The future expression requires a qualified complete WUP path; otherwise the historical prior is continued.

The surface prior has fixed coefficient 1 before correction. Annual WUP values include interpolation/projection of source epochs; they are not independently measured annual GFA.

4. Specify post-2020 continuation

After 2020, period coordinates remain historical summaries. Annual coordinates are seeded from their final three historical years:

xseedi = [1, Di, Pi, Ji, mean2018:2020(Ai,t), mean2018:2020(Ui,t)]T dt = 1   (t ≤ 2020);   dt = 0.8t−2020   (t > 2020) x̃i,t = κidtxseedi,   t > 2020

The damping applies to the factor correction, including its intercept, and to the non-residential contrast. It does not reduce the GFA stock by 20% per year. The separate hi,t prior continues to contribute.

This frozen B0 intake does not ingest fresh annual GDP, investment, lights or observed shocks after 2020. The same growth equations are used for retrospective post-2020 estimation, the 2026 nowcast and 2027–2030 conditional forecasts.

5. Estimate the seven coefficients

The model is fitted to 53 non-anchor, source-native relative-change observations from Chile, Germany, Denmark, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, South Korea and Romania. Each reference channel is normalized to its own 2020 value:

ysi,t = log(Referencesi,t / Referencesi,2020) rsi,t(β) = [ŷsi,t(β) − ysi,t] / 0.05 s identifies the matching residential or Total reference channel. The predicted Total ratio uses the sum of the component stocks, rather than treating Total and residential as interchangeable labels.

Residuals are grouped by country. The working precision matrix Pi accounts for the common 2020 denominator and budgets source/channel contributions so that each country's total precision trace is 1. Let ni(β) = √[riTPiri]. The objective is:

β^=argminβ[∑iρ1.35(ni(β))+12βTΛβ] Λ = diag(1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 9)
ρδ(n) = n² / 2n ≤ δρδ(n) = δ(n − δ/2)n > δ; δ = 1.35

The ridge penalty limits coefficient magnitude, with stronger shrinkage on the non-residential contrast. The 0.05 residual scale, robust threshold, pooling weights and penalty are fixed fitting settings.

6. Integrate growth into GFA stocks

Let Ai,2020 be the fixed TEMPO-converted Total anchor and qi the initial conditional non-residential share:

Ri,2020 = (1 − qi)Ai,2020 NRi,2020 = qiAi,2020

For either internal component S ∈ {R, NR}, accumulation from the anchor is:

Si,t=Si,2020exp(−∑u=t+12020gi,uS)t < 2020 Si,t=Si,2020exp(∑u=2021tgi,uS)t > 2020 Totali,t = Ri,t + NRi,t ΔTotali,t = Totali,t − Totali,t−1

Equivalently, Si,t = Si,t−1exp(gSi,t). Backcasting reverses these multipliers; nowcasting and forecasting apply them forward.

These equations describe the internal growth states. The adopted Atlas applies a separate GHSL-based sector reallocation that preserves every country-year Total and the 2020 sector anchors. The anchor and split remain estimates; this model does not separately identify gross construction and demolition.
