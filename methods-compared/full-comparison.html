Full numerical comparison

The first table retains all 48 procedures scored on the shared development panel. Some rows are alternative settings, controls or procedures that selected no added correction; they are not 48 independent model architectures.

The second table preserves 13 other records with different evaluation designs or unavailable scores. They must not be ranked directly against the shared-panel table. Blank results mean an evaluation is unavailable, not zero error.

Methods explained and current research choice

All scores are in percentage points. Annual and five-year errors compare estimated area increments with official-reference increments, divided by the official starting stock. They are not percentage errors in absolute national GFA. Lower mean absolute error and smaller absolute bias are better.

Annual results cover Denmark, Finland, Japan, South Korea and the Netherlands: 44 intervals, with 23 ending by 2020 and 21 after 2020. Pooled five-year results cover four countries and 20 overlapping windows, including windows crossing 2020. Country averages receive equal weight.

The numerical targets are annual error ≤0.5, absolute annual bias ≤0.2, and five-year error ≤2 percentage points. Meeting these numbers on this development panel does not establish independent Spec acceptance. Reference definitions and dates retain their documented scope; global absolute-GFA and genuine forecast accuracy are not established.

“Nested” means settings are chosen using only the training countries before the excluded country is scored. “Fixed” means the setting is specified in advance. Country-excluded fitting does not make this repeatedly used development panel an untouched final test.
