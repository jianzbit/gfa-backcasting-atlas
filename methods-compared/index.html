Methods compared

We compared different ways to estimate annual floorspace change while keeping the estimated 2020 starting stock fixed. The tests include curved factor responses, small decision trees, Gaussian-process regression, later satellite evidence, population-and-income demand, and constant or time-varying corrections. Shared rules use each country’s own inputs and starting stock.

The most suitable research option on the current development panel remains B0 for 2015–2020, followed by fixed-central saturating demand after 2020. This keeps the stronger backcast and improves the post-2020 path. Saturation means that income has a gradually weaker effect on per-person demand. “Fixed central” refers to a shared setting, not identical growth in every country. The continuation to 2030 is a conditional projection.

Why retain this combination?

The retained combination has an overall annual error of 0.4633 percentage points and a pooled five-year error of 1.7195 percentage points. The household-demand version has a lower pooled five-year error (1.2789), but a worse nowcast annual error (0.5307 versus 0.4999). The latest complete Bayesian tests do not improve the retained combination, so their extra complexity is not currently justified by this panel.

This combination was selected after inspecting development results. It is a research choice, not an independently confirmed global winner. Published B0 remains the operational baseline.

Reading the scores

All scores are in percentage points. Annual and five-year errors compare estimated area increments with official-reference increments, divided by the official starting stock. They are not percentage errors in absolute national GFA. Lower mean absolute error and smaller absolute bias are better.

Annual results cover Denmark, Finland, Japan, South Korea and the Netherlands: 44 intervals, with 23 ending by 2020 and 21 after 2020. Pooled five-year results cover four countries and 20 overlapping windows, including windows crossing 2020. Country averages receive equal weight.

The numerical targets are annual error ≤0.5, absolute annual bias ≤0.2, and five-year error ≤2 percentage points. Meeting these numbers on this development panel does not establish independent Spec acceptance. Reference definitions and dates retain their documented scope; global absolute-GFA and genuine forecast accuracy are not established.

“Nested” means settings are chosen using only the training countries before the excluded country is scored. “Fixed” means the setting is specified in advance. Country-excluded fitting does not make this repeatedly used development panel an untouched final test.
