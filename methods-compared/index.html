Current provisional choice: TEMPO 2020 + official GCAM residential / commercial functions

Selected provisionally for its published functions and externally calibrated regional parameters. This version does not fit new response coefficients or a simplified K to our seven-country panel. It is not the lowest-scoring development model.

185 complete country paths for 2015–2026. Regional density / income distributions and four donor-based starting shares remain declared proxies. Scientific acceptance and global accuracy are not established.
