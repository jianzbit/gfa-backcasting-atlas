4. Residential / Non-residential Split

Indicator 1.1 · Adopted Atlas allocation · GHS-BUILT-V R2023A · 2015–2030

Allocate the existing annual Total GFA using relative changes in residential and non-residential building-volume proxies. Preserve the 2020 sector anchors and every annual Total. This is a deterministic allocation with zero newly fitted composition parameters.

1. Define the inputs and the 2020 anchor

For country i and year t, let Ti,t be the frozen Total GFA from the growth model. Let Ri,2020 and Ni,2020 be its inherited residential and non-residential 2020 estimates:

qi,0 = Ni,2020 / Ti,2020Ri,2020 + Ni,2020 = Ti,2020

The initial share is the retained conditional 2020 allocation: a qualified non-residential volume share under an equal-conversion assumption, or the existing shared peer-median fallback. This step does not refit that anchor.

GHSL supplies modeled Total and non-residential building volumes at the native epochs 2015, 2020, 2025 and 2030. Construct the residential complement:

VRi,e = VTotali,e − VNi,e,   e ∈ {2015, 2020, 2025, 2030}

V is building volume in m³, not floor area. The non-residential class represents predominant building use; its complement does not identify every floor's actual use in a mixed-use building. The product uses fixed 2018 height information. 2025/2030 are projected source epochs.

2. Interpolate each sector's volume separately

Between adjacent five-year epochs a and b, define w = (t − a)/(b − a). For either sector s ∈ {R, N}:

Vsi,t = exp[(1 − w) log Vsi,a + w log Vsi,b]Used when both endpoints are positive.Vsi,t = (1 − w)Vsi,a + wVsi,bUsed when an endpoint is a structural zero. Exact native endpoints are preserved.

Use the qualified 100 m historical pair where available; otherwise use the qualified 1 km historical pair. Post-2020 transfer uses a qualified 1 km 2020/2025/2030 triplet. Each period is normalized to its own source-grid 2020 values; a different grid cannot silently revise the inherited 2020 GFA split.

Do not interpolate across a missing native epoch or extrapolate a missing endpoint. Annual interpolation is not an annual observation of building-use change.

3. Transfer relative volume changes into shares

mNi,t = VNi,t / VNi,2020mRi,t = VRi,t / VRi,2020 qi,t=qi,0mi,tNqi,0mi,tN+(1−qi,0)mi,tR

If both sectors grow proportionately, their shares stay unchanged. If the non-residential volume index grows faster, q increases. At 2020 both indices equal 1, so qi,2020 = qi,0.

This transfers relative sector changes; it does not directly replace the inherited share with VNi,t/VTotali,t. Interpreting relative volume changes as relative GFA changes is the allocation assumption.

Ni,t = Ti,t × qi,tRi,t = Ti,t − Ni,tRi,t + Ni,t = Ti,t

The Total path is taken from the growth model and is never multiplied by the GHSL volume indices here. The allocation replaces the previous sector path; it does not add another vote for Total growth.

5. Qualification, fallback and interpretation

195 country series × 16 years. Qualified relative-volume changes support 191 historical country paths and 189 post-2020 paths. Every country-year Total and every 2020 R/NR/Total anchor is preserved.

  • Required epochs must be available on a consistent source grid and country boundary, with qualified common valid support. Volumes must be finite, nonnegative and satisfy Total volume ≥ NR volume.
  • Both source 2020 sector volumes must be positive to form the relative indices. A zero reference component is not replaced by a small positive number.
  • Without qualified transfer support, retain that year's original B0 residential/non-residential values. This is an existing dynamic fallback path, not an invented zero or an automatically fixed share.
  • 2015/2020 source epochs are modeled historical estimates; intermediate historical years are interpolated. 2025/2030 source epochs are projections; intervening post-2020 years inherit those assumptions.

The conservation checks establish correct allocation arithmetic. They do not establish independent residential/non-residential GFA accuracy. The GHSL volume classes used to construct the split cannot independently validate that split.
